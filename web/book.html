✓ Downloading Worldline.dmg — then:

  1. Open Worldline.dmg and drag Worldline onto Applications.
  2. Open Worldline. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app (it isn't from the App Store) — click Done. Worldline is free and open source, so it isn't registered with Apple's paid program.
  3. System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway. Only once — after that it opens normally and updates itself.
Prefer Terminal? One line installs it and skips steps 2–3 (Terminal downloads aren't flagged):
curl -fsSL https://bradenseefeldt.github.io/worldline/install.sh | sh
